How it works

Three steps, no mystery. You can stop at any of them, and nothing becomes a policy until the carrier approves your application and you e-sign it yourself.

  1. 1

    We do the research first

    We study public records and New York filings for shops in your trade and class: rate changes, group programs, entity filings. No forms, no obligation, before any contact.

  2. 2

    You get a written package

    One page of findings specific to your shop, then the math and the sources behind each one. What we cannot know is stated plainly. Fifteen minutes on the phone verifies it.

  3. 3

    Apply online, e-sign yourself

    A ten-field application starts the carrier paperwork. You supply your own payroll and history, review the final terms, and e-sign. The carrier decides, and premium goes to the carrier.

Brand-new business with no prior workers comp? New employers can often move much faster than established shops that are switching carriers, because there are no prior loss records to collect. Ask us about the new-employer path when you call.
