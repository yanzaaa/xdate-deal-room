Workers Compensation - class split

New York treats plumbing inside buildings and burner work as two different classes, and the loss costs are meaningfully apart. Most shops that do both have never been asked which bucket their burner hours actually fall into.

See the math and the source

NY rates plumbing inside buildings at class 5183 (loss cost 3.519 per $100 of payroll, eff. 10/1/2026) and oil or gas burner install and service at 5193 (5.647). The spread is 60% on the same dollar of payroll. Which bucket your burner hours sit in decides real money, and no public record shows the split.

Workers Compensation - rate environment

New York comp loss costs came down 21.9% overall effective October 1st, and the plumbing class specifically fell 19.4%. If a renewal after that date did not come down, there is usually a findable reason.
