Why benefits are the retention tool in the trades

No survey required. Reason it from first principles and it holds on any jobsite in Nassau or Suffolk.

Poaching is a phone call

A trained tech's number gets around. When the only thing holding someone is the hourly rate, a competitor can beat it in one conversation, because wages are the easiest part of an offer to match.

Benefits raise the cost of leaving

When a family is on the group plan, walking away means new premiums, new deductibles, possibly new doctors, and a gap in between. That friction works quietly in your favor every month your people stay.

The job posting reads differently

Health, dental, and vision on the listing change who applies in the first place. You stop competing on rate alone and start competing on the whole job, which is a contest a good shop can win.

The statutory floor is already yours

New York requires DBL and PFL from private employers whether or not you ever add a group health plan. The Workers Compensation Board explains the employer obligations here. Getting the mandatory lines right is the floor; the package is what you build on it.

What a small-group package typically includes

Four building blocks. Two are choices that do the retention work, and two are New York obligations you carry either way.

The anchor

Group health

The centerpiece of the package and the line employees ask about first. Plan design, network, and how the contribution is split between shop and employee do most of the retention work.

Visible adds

Dental and vision

Small next to health, and disproportionately visible to employees and their families. Often the difference between a package that reads as complete and one that reads as bare minimum.

Required in NY

DBL, statutory disability

Short-term disability benefits for off-the-job injury and illness, mandatory for private NY employers. It is not the same policy as workers compensation, which responds to work-related injuries. Employer overview at wcb.ny.gov.

Required in NY

PFL, Paid Family Leave

New York's statutory paid family leave benefit, also mandatory for private employers and typically administered alongside DBL. Bonding with a new child, caring for a sick family member, and certain military family needs are the core uses.

How group renewals work

Group benefits run on an annual cycle. Between renewals a group is largely committed to its plan year, so the one date that matters is the renewal, and everything useful is planned backward from it.

  1. 1

    Census

    A simple roster: who is on the team, ages, home zip codes, and how the current plan is paid for, if there is one. It comes from you, never from a guess, and it is the input every market answer depends on.

  2. 2

    Market review at renewal

    When the renewal offer arrives, it gets compared against the alternatives while the window to act is open. Some years the incumbent offer is the right answer, and an honest review says so.

  3. 3

    Enrollment

    Employees see the options, make their own elections, and e-sign them personally. The new plan year starts on the renewal date, and the cycle rests until next year.

Not sure when your group renews, or whether you have a group plan at all? That is a fine place to start. Tell us what you know and we will help you find the date.

Benefits questions, answered plainly

Four that come up in nearly every first conversation. The full question and answer page goes deeper across every line.

Is DBL the same thing as workers compensation?

No, and mixing them up is common. Workers compensation responds to work-related injury and illness, and New York requires it for virtually every employer from the first employee. DBL is statutory short-term disability for off-the-job injury and illness, and PFL is paid family leave; both are also mandatory for private NY employers. Different obligations, different policies. Our workers comp page takes the first one in depth, and the distinction between liability lines is walked through in this plain-English comparison.

Not yet, and we will not pretend otherwise. The Life, Accident and Health licence application is in process. What we can do now is educational: map your statutory DBL and PFL obligations, learn your renewal date, and prepare the groundwork so the real conversation is ready at your next renewal. If the timing does not work for your shop, we will say so plainly.

When is the right time to talk about benefits?

At your group's renewal, which comes once a year. Between renewals a group is largely committed to its plan year, so the honest advice is to time the conversation to that date and start the homework about 90 days out. If you have no group plan today, there is no renewal to wait for, and the conversation can start whenever you are ready.
