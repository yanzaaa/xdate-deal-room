Before you press submit, know your rights

Your figures, your file

Payroll, receipts, and loss history come from you, typed by you. We never populate them, and nobody honest will. The carrier reconciles payroll at audit, so your own books are the right source.

You sign, nobody signs for you

NY DFS guidance is blunt: the applicant signs personally, every time. If any agent ever offers to "just sign it for you", that is your cue to leave.

Premium goes to the carrier

Always paid to the insurance carrier, never to us. An invoice from the carrier with the carrier's own payment instructions is the only bill you should ever see.
